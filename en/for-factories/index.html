You bring the product. We bring it to US customers.

Here's what we ask for before we build a store, and what we set up once we have it.

Is this for you?

  • You design and manufacture your own products.
  • You want to sell to US customers directly or through a US partner.
  • You can supply product media and specs.
  • You're comfortable with customers reserving with a deposit.

What you provide

  • Studio product photos, all angles and what's in the box
  • Photos of the product installed or in use
  • Power-on and feature videos (for electronics)
  • Night or lit shots, if the product lights up
  • Install video or manual
  • Spec sheet: materials, size, weight, power, certifications you actually hold
  • Fitment list (for auto parts)
  • Packaging size and weight
  • Written OK for us to use the media

We'll share the full spec list once we start working together.

What we set up

  • A US brand website
  • Checkout where customers reserve with a deposit
  • A separate Stripe account we open for the brand
  • Customer service
  • A US business address in Commerce, CA.

How it works

  1. 01
    Talk. Tell us about your product and which model you're considering.
  2. 02
    Agree. We confirm the model and terms in writing.
  3. 03
    Send media. You supply photos, videos, specs and fitment. We don't shoot product media.
  4. 04
    We build the store. A brand site with its own checkout and its own Stripe account.
  5. 05
    Go live. Customers reserve with a deposit; we handle the US side.

FAQ

Who owns the customer data?

Agreed case by case, in writing.

Is it exclusive?

Agreed case by case.

How long until a store is live?

It depends on the product and the media; timelines are agreed case by case.

Do you shoot product photos?

No. You supply all media; we use it on the site.
