How we work

Three models. One clear split of work.

In every model you make the product and we run the US store. What changes is whose brand it is and who sells it.

01
MODEL 01

We operate your brand.

Factories that already have a brand and want it sold direct to US customers.

StoreCheckoutSupport

You provide

Brand assets, product media, specs, product supply.

We run

The brand's US website, checkout, customer service, US business address.

Payment

Customers reserve with a deposit on a separate Stripe account we open for your brand. Revenue split and fees are agreed case by case.

02
MODEL 02

We distribute.

Factories that want a US dealer instead of running the brand themselves.

You provide

Product, media, dealer pricing.

We run

Listing and selling your products in the US under your brand.

Payment

Sales run on a separate Stripe account we open for your brand. Dealer terms and exclusivity are agreed case by case.

03
MODEL 03

We launch our own brand.

Factories with a strong product but no brand for the US market.

You provide

Product, media, specs.

We run

A new brand under OLI & CECE: name, site, checkout, customer service. Tachi Club and HECCEI are ours.

Payment

Customers reserve with a deposit on the new brand's own Stripe account. Supply terms are agreed case by case.

Payments

Deposit first, accounts kept separate

Customers reserve with a deposit. Each brand runs on its own Stripe account, opened and run by us, so payments, payouts and records stay separate.

Limits

What we don't do

We don't shoot product photos or videos.

We don't hold large inventory up front.

We don't mix brands' payments in one account.
