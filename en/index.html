How it works

  1. 01
    Talk. Tell us about your product and which model you're considering.
  2. 02
    Agree. We confirm the model and terms in writing.
  3. 03
    Send media. You supply photos, videos, specs and fitment. We don't shoot product media.
  4. 04
    We build the store. A brand site with its own checkout and its own Stripe account.
  5. 05
    Go live. Customers reserve with a deposit; we handle the US side.

How we keep it simple

Deposit first.

Customers reserve with a deposit, so no one carries large stock up front.

Light on assets.

We put work into the store, not into warehouses full of unsold stock.

One brand, one Stripe account.

Each brand's payments run on its own account, kept separate.

You own the media.

You supply the product photos and videos; we put them to work.
